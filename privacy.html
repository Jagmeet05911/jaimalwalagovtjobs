Privacy Policy

This website provides public recruitment information. Job searches and saved-job preferences may be stored in your browser. The administrator area uses a secure authentication service and a hosted database to manage job records.

For basic traffic measurement, this website stores a random pseudonymous browser identifier in local storage and records the visit date and page path in its database. The public site displays an approximate total of unique browser visits per day; detailed page-view statistics are available only to the administrator. This is not a count of verified individual people, and clearing browser storage or using another browser/device may count the same person again.

Do not enter sensitive personal information into this website. When you follow an external link, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply.

This is a basic privacy notice for this information portal. If analytics, advertising, or additional third-party services are added, this page should be updated to describe them accurately.

For privacy questions, contact jaimalwalajobpoint@gmail.com.
